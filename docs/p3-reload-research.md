# Persona 3 Reload source research

This catalog records published main-campaign calendars and supporting references inspected on 7 September 2026. It supports a factual knowledge base for a flexible companion. It does not establish a tested 100% route or a released Persona 3 Reload app.

The machine-readable [source catalog](../knowledge/p3-reload/sources.json) contains exact URLs, observed metadata, route lineage, chapter links, and inspection limits. Dates describe the page inspected. An updated hub does not establish that every chapter received the same update.

## Method and scope

Discovery covered the GameFAQs Reload guide index, major completion-guide publishers, Steam Community, and targeted web searches. The audit inspected introductions, bylines, version labels, chapter indexes, final-month content, and attribution where available. Collaborative-browser inspection supplied pages that search extraction could not read reliably.

The catalog contains 17 discovered calendar or walkthrough candidates and four supplemental references. It includes partial and unverified candidates so later research does not mistake a search result for a complete route. It does not claim to contain every guide online. Japanese walkthrough discovery and video-only playthroughs remain outside this pass.

Original Persona 3, FES, Portable, and Episode Aigis schedules are excluded. Some authors also maintain those guides, and some Reload pages link to Episode Aigis. Their presence does not extend this dataset's scope. Shared publishers, authors, mirrors, and legacy versions do not establish independent agreement.

## Extraction boundaries

The catalog identifies source structure without storing complete routes. PSNProfiles uses many HTML tables. Lukaight's English calendar is one large table-heavy page. Game8 provides separate monthly pages with daily tables. PowerPyx and RPG Site use dated nested lists. The AVE page uses headings and lists, while Steam also uses table-like divs. A table count includes unrelated page content and is not a count of calendar days.

Published route instructions contain choices made by the author. A chosen Social Link date does not establish its opening date or deadline. A monthly target rank does not establish a game requirement. Complete source coverage does not establish that arbitrary deviations can still finish every activity.

The knowledge base stores short original factual statements with section locators and supporting URLs. It separates constraints, route choices, and editorial recommendations. Source conflicts belong in the dispute register. No full daily schedule, dialogue transcript, or walkthrough prose is reproduced here.

## Reliability limits

No guide received an independent full-game playthrough during this audit. Author-reported verification remains attributed to the author. Uncredited similarity requires investigation before treating two sources as independent.

Known reference conflicts include missing-person dates in RPG Site versus GameFAQs and Game8 Japanese. The lead records field-level decisions in the knowledge base. Old reader comments can identify possible errors, but they do not establish that the current page still contains those errors.

The availability calendar's omissions, older guide versions, and partially completed walkthroughs prevent a blanket claim that all dates are verified. The companion remains unpublished until calendar exceptions, prerequisites, missed-event behavior, and playthrough validation meet the release criteria in the [knowledge-base README](../knowledge/p3-reload/README.md).

P5 Tracker

Plan your month without a daily walkthrough

A day-by-day walkthrough is useful when you want an exact route. If you prefer choosing how to spend each afternoon, a monthly checklist gives you a smaller set of goals to work toward.

Use the checklist alongside your game

  1. Select your current in-game month in the tracker.
  2. Review the monthly objectives and date-specific tasks before spending your next time slot.
  3. Mark completed tasks as you play, then check the remaining goals when you return.

P5 Tracker carries eligible unfinished tasks forward and hides expired date-specific events. Check deadlines before advancing the game. A tracker cannot undo a missed in-game opportunity.

Keep your playthrough yours

You choose the order of activities. The calendar brings monthly objectives together with your recorded progress. It does not simulate every possible route or guarantee a perfect run.

What about spoilers?

The app includes character names, objectives and dungeon information. Stay on your current month to limit what you see, but do not treat it as a spoiler-free guide.

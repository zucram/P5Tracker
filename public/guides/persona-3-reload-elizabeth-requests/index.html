Start dates, prerequisites and item opportunities

The start date is the earliest request notice. Earlier reports, story progress, item access and Tartarus barriers can delay completion. A request can appear before its required floor opens.

Report-by dates are deadlines. Item opportunity dates identify a purchase or collection chance and are not reporting deadlines. If an opportunity has passed, an item already in your inventory can still satisfy a request with no fixed reporting deadline.

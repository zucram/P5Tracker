Recruit it before it flees

  1. Use the listed damage type to knock it down. Choose a weaker attack if a powerful hit would defeat it.
  2. During Hold Up, talk to the Treasure Demon to recruit it instead of choosing an All-out Attack. These encounters do not use the usual personality-answer negotiation.
  3. Record the capture in your registry. If it cannot join, check Joker's level and available Persona stock slots.

Treasure Demons are fusion materials; Joker cannot equip them for battle. They are useful for inherited skills and Treasure Demon fusion combinations.

Track captured Treasure Demons

The tracker opens with Treasure Demons only. Check off those you have recruited; the checkmarks save in your browser. Uncheck the filter to return to the full registry.

Why does a fusion calculator give me a different result?

Treasure Demon fusion uses the other Persona's current level and the enabled DLC list. Check both in the Royal fusion calculator. The levels in this table identify the Treasure Demons, rather than specifying a fusion recipe.

Does this list work for the original Persona 5?

No. Several affinities changed in Royal, and Orichalcum is a Royal addition. Use a reference for your specific edition.

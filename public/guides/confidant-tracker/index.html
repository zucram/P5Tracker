Keep track of confidant ranks and social stat requirements

When you return to Persona 5 Royal after a break, remembering every relationship rank can be difficult. P5 Tracker keeps your recorded ranks in one place and shows monthly rank targets.

Check your next relationship goal

  1. Open Confidants and enter the ranks shown in your game.
  2. Record your social stat levels so the tracker can flag relevant rank requirements.
  3. Expand a confidant card when you need its dialogue or gift reference.

The tracker does not read your game save. Update ranks manually after you play. Monthly targets are planning guidance, not a guarantee that every relationship will be completed.

Use it on your second screen

Open the browser app on your phone while you play. No account is needed. Your progress is stored in that browser, so another device will not automatically have the same save.

Back up before switching devices

Use the app's Sync Terminal to export your progress and import it on the other device. Keep a backup before clearing browser data.

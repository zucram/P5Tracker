Fusion levels and recipe limits

Ordinary fusion uses the ingredients' base levels and Arcana, not their raised levels in your save. A result's listed level does not include Social Link experience bonuses.

The examples establish the resulting Persona. They do not automatically give it a requested inherited skill. Native skills and their learning levels appear in each Persona entry. Inherited skills still depend on the ingredients and the result's inheritance rules.

The result can require a Social Link, request, Linked Episode or Tartarus unlock. The calculator shows that condition alongside the recipe. Check the Velvet Room's level and fusion requirements in your game.

Persona 3 Reload calendar and Social Link tracker

Plan your next day without committing to one exact walkthrough. This free Persona 3 Reload companion combines a flexible April–January calendar, 22 Social Link rank trackers, Academics, Charm and Courage, and reminders for missable events.

A flexible alternative to a day-by-day walkthrough

Set your in-game date and choose daytime or evening. Record the Social Links you have started and your current ranks. The planner checks usual meeting days, reviewed school closures and stat requirements, then suggests a few options to check in-game. You choose how to spend the time.

A fixed day-by-day guide follows one route. This semi-daily planner adapts reminders to your recorded progress. It does not simulate affinity points, every story choice or remaining opportunities well enough to guarantee a 100% run.

Keep missable deadlines in view

See missing people in Tartarus, dated Elizabeth requests and Linked Episode windows. Mark completed events so they stop appearing as unfinished. Linked Episodes can require earlier events, and a window does not mean an invitation appears on every day inside it.

Save your own monthly plan

Add personal goals and keep a checklist for each month. Progress is saved in this browser, separately from the Royal tracker. Download a backup to move to another device; importing keeps a previous save for recovery. No account is required.

Beta scope and sources

This companion covers the main Persona 3 Reload campaign. Persona 3, FES, Portable and Episode Aigis use different systems and are not covered. Dates and requirements are reviewed against original player walkthroughs, with sources on the reference pages and event cards. Story choices and rank-specific availability still need checking in-game. Names are hidden by default in the interactive tracker, but dates, arcana and linked source pages can contain spoilers.

The interactive planner requires JavaScript. Both reference guides above work without it. The tracker is free and ad-free; optional tips do not unlock features.
